Type on your keyboard to talk to it; Enter sends, and it thinks and then hums an answer (or, with the whole mind here, speaks it). Arrow keys tilt it. Left alone for a while, its thoughts drift by as small text. The mind-driven parts run on 1,800 real pieces from Wink's own network (look/sketch/mind-states.js).
